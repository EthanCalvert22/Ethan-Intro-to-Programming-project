# M&A Email Templates

Source: Notion page "M&A Email Templates". Refresh with `nsp sync-prompts`.

Templates for post-call follow-ups. Style: friendly but direct, honest, formal and articulate sentences, no em dashes, concise. Anything covered on the call is framed as already discussed. Fill every [bracket] from the transcript, Ethan's notes and research on the company and sector.

## 1. Strong lead (likely $10M+ and selling)

Attach the NDA (Word) unless already signed.

Subject: Nassau Street Partners | Following up on today's call

Hi [Name],

Thank you for your time today. I appreciated how openly you spoke about [the business and the reasons behind exploring a sale], and it gave me a clear picture of what you have built.

[Specific insight from the transcript and research, for example: The combination of recurring revenue and a defensible position in [niche] is exactly what strategic acquirers in [sector] are paying for at present, particularly following [recent deal or trend].]

I will be candid on one point: [the honest question a buyer will raise]. It is not a concern about the opportunity, rather something we would want to address early when positioning it.

As discussed, the next step on our side is to review the opportunity with Gary Shields, Bill Snyder and Ron Reich, who lead our M&A transactions. I have attached our mutual NDA. Once it is signed, it would be helpful to receive [overview or deck, the last three years of financials and the current year forecast], and we will come back to you as soon as possible regarding time with them.

As mentioned, these two short videos give a good sense of how Gary views the current market:
M&A Outlook 2026: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4PoEY9uzD14
Gary Shields on AI, M&A and Market Conditions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DI6z0cAAxMQ

Best regards,
Ethan

## 2. Promising, more information needed

Attach the NDA if figures are commercially sensitive.

Subject: Nassau Street Partners | Following up on today's call

Hi [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak today. [One line on what stood out about the business.]

To be straightforward, the opportunity looks interesting, but before I take it to our senior M&A team I need a clearer view of the numbers. In particular, [revenue and EBITDA for the last two to three years, ownership, and anything specific raised on the call] would allow me to give you an honest view of how buyers are likely to value the business.

I have attached our mutual NDA so that you can share these in confidence. Once I have them, I will review them promptly and come back to you with my thoughts and the next steps.

Best regards,
Ethan

## 4. Too small for us

Subject: Nassau Street Partners | Following up on today's call

Hi [Name],

Thank you for your time today and for being so open about the business.

I want to be honest with you rather than leave things open-ended. Our M&A process is built around businesses that would typically sell for $10M or more, and at its current size I do not believe we would be the right advisers to deliver the outcome you deserve. [Optional: a firm that specialises in transactions of your size, such as a business broker, is likely to serve you better.]

That said, [one genuine positive about the business]. If it reaches [scale they mentioned] in the future, I would be glad to revisit the conversation.

Best regards,
Ethan
